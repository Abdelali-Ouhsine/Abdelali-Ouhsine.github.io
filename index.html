Marketing analytics · Power BI + Python

Which campaigns, products and channels actually drive revenue?

The problem

A retailer ran 6 campaigns across 8 countries and 3 sales channels with no clear view of what worked.

What I did

  • Profiled 2,239 customers and 30+ features in Python
  • ANOVA, Chi-Square and Pearson tests to validate drivers
  • Star schema in Power Query with DAX measures
  • Interactive dashboard with country, month and campaign filters

Key findings

Wine spending is the #1 revenue lever (r = 0.89 with total spend).
Catalog outperforms web and store (r = 0.78 vs 0.52).
Campaign response is the same in every country (χ² p = 0.87).
Python · pandas · SciPy · statsmodels · Power BI · DAX

Economic analysis · Power BI

Measuring the economic cost of conflict.

The question

How do different conflict types affect GDP, unemployment, poverty, food insecurity and currency stability across regions?

What I did

  • Modeled conflict, region and economic indicators
  • Pre-war vs during-war comparisons for unemployment and poverty
  • Two-page report with map, KPI cards and conflict filters

What the data shows

Civil wars carry the largest share of war cost (36%) and during-war unemployment (42%).
